02 — HOW I APPROACH DESIGN

How I approach design

I try to make the reasoning visible—not just the final screen.

01

Understand

I start by understanding the problem, users, business context and constraints.

02

Explore

I organize research, identify patterns and explore possible directions.

03

Structure

I turn insights into user flows, information architecture and wireframes.

04

Design

I develop the interface through hierarchy, typography, layout and visual systems.

05

Refine

I test, review and iterate until the experience becomes clearer and easier to use.
